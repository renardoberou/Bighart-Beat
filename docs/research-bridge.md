# Research Bridge — Bighart Beat

Core idea: groove is structured surprise that recovers into meter.

## MVP metrics
- Syncopation
- Meter confidence / anchor
- Surprise / tension
- Recoverability
- Movement-drive proxy

## User-facing labels
SYNC, ANCHOR, TENSION, RECOVER, DRIVE.

## Avoid
Do not claim to measure brains, dopamine, pleasure, therapy, free energy, or neural entrainment. Keep it as musical proxy metrics inspired by rhythm cognition.
